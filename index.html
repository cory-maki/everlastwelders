About the Company

Built Around the Operator

Everlast Welders (Everlast Power Products Inc.) started in San Francisco in 2004 as a small online business. It has since grown into a multinational company with distributorships in multiple countries, supplying welding and cutting equipment for work in home garages, professional shops, and worksites.

Development at Everlast begins with the person using the machine. Operator feedback informs design decisions before engineers finalize performance requirements, an approach set out in the company’s product development priorities. In 2008, that process produced the first-generation Power series of IGBT inverter welders and plasma cutters.

Manufacturing takes place offshore with name-brand electronic components from suppliers including Infineon, Texas Instruments, and Panasonic, while customer service, inventory, and product servicing are staffed in the United States.
